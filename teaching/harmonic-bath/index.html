An oscillator with memory

A moving oscillator disturbs its surroundings. Their delayed response changes how it slows down.

Parameters

Reduced units: mass = 1 and initial displacement = 1. Frequency and γ have units of inverse time; τ has units of time.

1 · Harmonic bath

Many oscillators form the surroundings

Our oscillator is attached to a continuum of harmonic bath modes. Each mode responds to its motion. Adding their responses gives a force that depends on earlier velocities.

x¨(t)=−ω02x(t)−∫0tK(t−s)x˙(s)ds+ξ(t)

ω₀ sets the spring frequency. K describes the memory of past motion. ξ is the fluctuating force from the bath.

We plot the mean motion after release from rest at x = 1. The bath starts in thermal equilibrium around that held position. Its fluctuating force averages to zero. The spring includes the usual bath counterterm, keeping ω₀ fixed as the coupling changes.

2 · Memory in time

How long does a velocity matter?

Choose an exponential memory. A velocity from τ time units ago receives about 37% of the weight of the current velocity.

K(t)=γτe−t/τ,t≥0

The area under K is γ. Increasing τ spreads the same area over a longer time. As τ approaches zero, the friction force approaches −γv.

Analytical result and calculation

The exponential kernel is the Drude harmonic-bath model. Henkel (2021), Appendix A.2, Eqs. A12–A14 gives its transform, spectrum, and memory kernel. The plots evaluate these formulas with unit mass.

How the motion is calculated

Introduce the accumulated friction z. The mean motion obeys three linear equations:

ẋ = v, v̇ = −ω₀²x − z, τż = γv − z

Starting from (x, v, z) = (1, 0, 0), the solution is y(t) = exp(At)y(0). The tool evaluates this matrix exponential. Equivalently, the Laplace transform of the displacement is

Temperature and fluctuations

In the classical model, ⟨ξ(t)ξ(s)⟩ = kBT K(|t−s|). Temperature controls the fluctuations around the mean. For this linear system, the mean motion shown above follows the same equations at every temperature. At equilibrium the average total oscillator energy is kBT.
