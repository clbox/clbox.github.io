Methodology

Structure generation. We searched cells containing one or two MePTCDI molecules. SAMPLE enumerated commensurate supercells of DFT-optimised bilayer hBN. GenSec generated rigid, flat molecular configurations with independently sampled in-plane rotations (0–359°), fractional lateral positions and centre-of-mass heights (3.2–3.6 Å above the uppermost hBN atom). Additional starts perturbed promising packings. Periodic intermolecular contacts shorter than 1.5 Å (H–H), 1.7 Å (H–heavy atom) or 2.1 Å (heavy–heavy atom) were rejected, as were structures with any molecular atom less than 2.5 Å above the slab.

MACE screening. We scored 6,788 generated structures using MACE-MH-1, head omat_pbe, with PBE-D3(BJ) pairwise dispersion. Candidates were selected by energy, cell diversity and seeded exploratory sampling. ASE FIRE produced 95 relaxation endpoints: the initial 48 used a 30-step limit and 0.10 eV/Å force-norm target; the 23 motif-comparison runs used 100 steps and 0.05 eV/Å; each of three extension batches used eight runs, 60 steps and 0.05 eV/Å.

Full-pool relaxation. After removing 16 exact duplicate inputs, we relaxed all 6,875 remaining records, including the controls and earlier endpoints, with ASE FIRE and the same MACE + D3(BJ) model in float64. All reached a maximum molecular force below 0.05 eV/Å. Both hBN layers and the cell stayed fixed. Different starts can reach the same endpoint. The relaxed viewer retains the original point IDs and labels DFT selection of the starting structures separately.

Round 1. The density extension generated 143 starts (seed 2026110301). The catalogue search generated 1,004 starts across 140 declared symmetry-distinct cells at 0.70–0.90 molecules/nm², minimum width 7 Å and aspect ratio ≤3 (seed 2026110302). Both used uniform azimuths (0–180°), the same contact checks, and at most 200,000 placement attempts per cell. All 1,147 endpoints are now converged and included.

Round 2. The same 140-cell catalogue and generation settings, with new deterministic per-cell seeds, produced 1,045 starts. Of these, 1,043 converged and are included; two remain above the force threshold. Fifteen cells met the 50-start quota, 33 were partially filled and 92 produced no accepted starts. Unmet quotas remain recorded.

DFT refinement. Earlier tier-1 and light-basis relaxations supplied starting structures for full tight tier-2 reoptimisation. The DFT viewer contains only converged full tight tier-2 PBE+XDM results, with a 0.005 eV/Å force threshold and SCF thresholds of 10−7 eV for energy, 10−5 eV for the eigenvalue sum and 10−7 for density. In both MACE and DFT relaxations, molecular atoms move freely while the cell and both hBN layers remain fixed. Adsorption energies are (Einterface − Eslab)/N − Emolecule, using matching references.
