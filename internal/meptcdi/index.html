Methodology

Structure generation. We searched cells containing one or two MePTCDI molecules. SAMPLE enumerated commensurate supercells of DFT-optimised bilayer hBN. GenSec generated rigid, flat molecular configurations with independently sampled in-plane rotations (0–359°), fractional lateral positions and centre-of-mass heights (3.2–3.6 Å above the uppermost hBN atom). Additional starts perturbed promising packings. Periodic intermolecular contacts shorter than 1.5 Å (H–H), 1.7 Å (H–heavy atom) or 2.1 Å (heavy–heavy atom) were rejected, as were structures with any molecular atom less than 2.5 Å above the slab.

MACE screening. We scored 6,788 generated structures using MACE-MH-1, head omat_pbe, with PBE-D3(BJ) pairwise dispersion. Candidates were selected by energy, cell diversity and seeded exploratory sampling. ASE FIRE produced 95 relaxation endpoints: the initial 48 used a 30-step limit and 0.10 eV/Å force-norm target; the 23 motif-comparison runs used 100 steps and 0.05 eV/Å; each of three extension batches used eight runs, 60 steps and 0.05 eV/Å.

DFT refinement. The original 16 structures used PBE+XDM, a tier-1 basis with tight grids and a 0.02 eV/Å force threshold. A broader 40-structure light pass supplied representatives for full tight tier-2 reoptimisation at 0.005 eV/Å. The DFT viewer shows the original tier-1 and converged tight results separately labelled. In both MACE and DFT relaxations, molecular atoms move freely while the cell and both hBN layers remain fixed. Adsorption energies are (Einterface − Eslab)/N − Emolecule, using matching references.
