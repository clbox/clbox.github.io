Reading the maps

MACE and DFT energies are separate models, not interchangeable values. In the MACE viewer use batch, stage and status to distinguish starts, controls and relaxed structures. The latest batch is extension_003.

Of 95 relaxed MACE endpoints, 84 meet their stated thresholds and 11 are step-limited. Thresholds differ: 0.10 eV/Å for the initial dense pass and 0.05 for later batches. Related structures are retained, not symmetry-deduplicated.

The angle is the acute angle between molecular long axes, not the angle relative to hBN; it is zero for a one-molecule cell. Both hBN layers and the cell are fixed. Existing MACE point numbers, including 6428/6429, are preserved. This is a bounded search, not proof of a global minimum.
