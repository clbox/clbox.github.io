Connor Box is a Postdoctoral Research Fellow in the Department of Chemistry at the University of Warwick, working with Prof. Reinhard Maurer. His research focuses on theoretical and computational chemistry, particularly the development of methods to study nonadiabatic dynamics and electron-phonon interactions at surfaces.

Connor completed his PhD under the supervision of Prof. Reinhard Maurer at Warwick, where he worked on electronic friction and quantum dynamics in gas–surface scattering. He holds an MChem degree from the University of York.
